The Story of Frank Ilett's Hair Vow

💇 On October 5, 2024, Manchester United fan Frank Ilett (known online as The United Strand) made a bold vow: he would not cut his hair until United won 5 games in a row.

⏳ As of today, -- days later, Frank is still waiting. His hair grows longer with each disappointing result, and the Man United win streak remains elusive.

📊 Meanwhile, we've been tracking every other team in the world that has managed to achieve this seemingly impossible feat. Spoiler alert: it's a lot of teams.
